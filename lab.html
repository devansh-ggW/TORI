TEST MATRIX

Think like an annoying customer.

The Core should survive the ugly forms people actually type. Test short messages, missing punctuation, abbreviations, slang, mixed questions, product nicknames, and requests whose required fact is not configured.

Language noise

“hw much”, “whats ur price”, “kitne ka”, “buisness name?”, “plz send link”, “what ur product for”.

Mixed intent

“how much is it and when can i get it”, “is it available and can i order”, “what is it for and send me the link”.

Uncertainty

“can you do better”, “tomorrow possible?”, “that one?”, “what about the other one?” — these need context or configured rules.

Missing facts

Ask for a discount with no discount rule, availability with no stock field, or price with no price configured.

Entity ambiguity

Add two similar products and ask “how much is the vault”. The engine should not confidently pick one without enough evidence.

Formal output

Business-name questions should return business identity. Purpose questions should explain purpose. Contact questions should return contact details. Different intent, different answer.
