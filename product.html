The product is built around a simple premise: the business already knows most answers. ReplyFlix's job is to identify the question, identify what the question refers to, retrieve the right facts, and show whether the answer is sufficiently grounded before it is automated.

Business identity

Name, registered name, category, description, tagline, website, email, phone, WhatsApp, country, city, address and hours.

B / CATALOG

Many products

Each product gets a name, price, currency, aliases, purpose, contents, stock, delivery, link, refund detail and more.

C / SERVICES

Many services

Services can have their own aliases, scope, starting price, availability, link, delivery expectations and rules.

D / FACTS

Custom knowledge

Anything that does not fit the standard fields can become a custom fact with its own question aliases.
