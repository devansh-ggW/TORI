1. What cookies ReplyFlix uses

ReplyFlix currently uses an essential authentication/session cookie named rf_session. It helps keep an authenticated account signed in and is marked HttpOnly and Secure by the service backend.

2. Browser storage

The website may also use localStorage and sessionStorage for application state, authentication session fallback data and local-first operation. These are browser storage mechanisms rather than cookies.

3. No advertising cookies

The current ReplyFlix prototype does not intentionally use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

4. Third-party services

ReplyFlix uses Cloudflare infrastructure for hosting and backend services. Third-party providers may set or process identifiers only where needed to provide services or where their own functionality is enabled.

5. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking the essential session cookie may prevent ReplyFlix account authentication from working correctly.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when the website, authentication, analytics or third-party integrations change. The effective date above should be updated when this page changes.
