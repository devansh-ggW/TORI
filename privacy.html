1. Scope

This policy describes the current ReplyFlix website and account flow.

2. Account information

Email sign-up can process your email address, authentication identifiers, timestamps and optional name.

3. Age and eligibility

ReplyFlix requires users to be 18 or older. The account flow uses an age gate, and the account record stores the date of birth and eligibility state needed to enforce that requirement.

4. Knowledge and messages

Business profile information, products, services, facts, keywords and learned phrases may be synchronized to the authenticated ReplyFlix account in Cloudflare D1. Business-related customer messages stored in the Messages section are also stored in Cloudflare D1 and are associated with the account that received them.

5. Cloudflare authentication

ReplyFlix authentication runs through a Cloudflare Worker backend. Cloudflare D1 stores the account, profile, knowledge and message records described in this policy.

6. Local and cloud storage

ReplyFlix may use browser storage for fast local operation and Cloudflare D1 storage for account-backed knowledge and message history. Clearing browser storage does not necessarily delete cloud account data. Actual deletion and retention practices should be documented before launch.

7. Cookies and browser storage

ReplyFlix currently uses an essential authentication/session cookie to keep signed-in sessions working. The website may also use localStorage and sessionStorage for local application state and authentication fallback data. The current prototype does not intentionally use advertising cookies.

8. Use and sharing

Information may be used to authenticate accounts, operate requested features, maintain security and enforce rules. Service providers may receive information needed to operate their part of the service, and disclosures may occur when legally required.

9. Retention and deletion

Local knowledge remains until cleared or overwritten. Exact account retention/deletion periods must match your actual Cloudflare settings and business practices and should be filled in before launch.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing. Contact [Privacy Email] for requests.

11. Changes and contact

Update this policy when data practices change. Privacy contact: [[Your Legal Business Name]], [Privacy Email], [Business Address].
